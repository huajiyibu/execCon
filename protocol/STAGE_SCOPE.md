# Frozen Stage Scope

Effective 2026-10-09, ExecCon v2 studies only the early-stage ALE agents expansion:

| Stage | Cumulative specialists |
|---|---:|
| h1 | 2 |
| h2 | 5 |
| h3 | 8 |

h4–h6 are excluded from the experiment design, statistics, and conclusions. Their current `missing agents bundle(s)` errors are platform scope information, not task failures and not a reason to block h1–h3 work.

The paper-facing claim is:

> Execution-aware convergence under early-stage agent-pool expansion from 2 to 5 to 8 specialists.

This is not presented as a complete six-stage EvoHarnessBench reproduction and does not infer behavior of the full 24-agent pool.
